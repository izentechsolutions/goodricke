High Quality Goodricke Tea

Goodricke owns 29 Tea estates across Darjeeling, Assam & Dooars. Many of its tea gardens are award winning and highly appreciated for its quality produce, and delicately hand-picking exclusive tea leaves. Other than producing season specific teas like Flush teas, moonlight teas, oolong teas, white teas, green teas, Darjeeling single estate exclusive teas, assam teas Goodricke also produces various unique tea flavours & blends.

Goodricke team is committed and highly concerned for best quality, perfect aroma, and delighting experience every time you sip Goodricke Tea.
