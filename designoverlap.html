Our gardens are cultivated the good way, our products are crafted the good way and our people set the shape the good way. Our legacy sets the standard and is what builds the good way. It is how we think, how we work, and how we show up every single day. Because if it carries the Goodricke name, it must be Made The Good Way.

The Good Stories

Female representation increased by over 5% in Goodricke's core operations

At Goodricke, gender equality is more than a commitment. It is an integral part of how we cultivate our people, strengthen our communities, and shape the future of our business. We believe that diverse perspectives foster stronger teams, better decisions, and more sustainable growth.

From empowering women in the workforce to supporting leadership development and inclusive workplace practices, our efforts are rooted in long-term impact. Between 2021-22, female representation across our core operations increased by nearly 5%, reflecting our steady progress toward building a more inclusive, equitable, and empowering workplace.

Two inclusive education centres opened for plantation communities

Education is the most enduring harvest. Our two inclusive education centres bring quality learning to children across our plantation communities.

Built with accessibility at their core, the centres serve every child - laying foundations that outlast any season.

Read More

Regenerating the landscapes that grow our tea

Healthy gardens begin with healthy ecosystems. Our stewardship programmes restore soil, shade and biodiversity across our estates.

From native tree planting to water conservation, regeneration is woven into daily estate practice.

Read More

Advancing TB care across our estate communities

Our estate health programmes bring screening, treatment and awareness to thousands of workers and their families.

Working with public health partners, we are helping drive early detection and complete recovery.

Read More

Preserving a centuries-old tea heritage

Some of our gardens have been in continuous cultivation for over 150 years - living archives of craft.

We preserve their heritage varieties and hand-processing traditions while investing in their future.

Strengthening the communities that make every cup possible

From housing to healthcare, our community programmes reach more than 38,000 people across our estates.

Shared prosperity is the good way - when our communities thrive, so does every leaf we grow.
